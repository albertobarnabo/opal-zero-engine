OpalZero
Logo Exploration · III · Colour + Form
01
Iridescent Wheel
Six arc-trapezoid segments, each a curved wedge (not a pie slice) with both edges arced. Three opal colours cycle — teal, blue, violet — like the play-of-colour shifting across a black opal as you tilt it.
02
Opal Ring
A bold solid ring — wall thickness 21 units — filled with a diagonal iridescent gradient. The zero as a material object. The colour shift is what gives it life; a plain ring would be inert.
03
Fire
The silhouette of a polished opal cabochon — pointed crown, rounded base. The gradient climbs through violet, blue and teal, mimicking the spectral fire seen in a black opal as light rakes across its surface.
04
Silica
Three spheres, just touching, in close-packed formation. A literal reference: black opal's iridescence is caused by close-packed silica nanospheres diffracting light. The stone's microstructure as its mark.
05
Opal Stone
A hexagon where every corner is a smooth curve — achieved by passing through edge midpoints and using vertices as bezier control points. The silhouette of a polished hexagonal gem. Gradient pours through it like light through stone.
06
Shield
A solid triangle with its inscribed circle precisely voided — the circle fits perfectly, touching all three sides. Triangle = precision and quality. The void circle = zero. Two concepts sharing one boundary.
